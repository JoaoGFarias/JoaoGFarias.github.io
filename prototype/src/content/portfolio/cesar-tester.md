Testing consultant for a global top-S&P-500 client's employee shift management platform — 300,000 employees in scope. Maintained the legacy PHP/Symfony system, then participated in the rebuild on Ember.js + Express.js + Kubernetes + Docker.

## Highlights

- **Testing frameworks from unit to E2E** with Selenium, Behat (PHP), and Ember.js's testing tools.
- **Performance testing** with Sitespeed.io.
- **API automation** with Postman.
- **Exploratory and deep testing** — bug analysis, root cause analysis, hypothesis-driven investigation.
- **Whole-team testing planning, BDD, and Scrum** — embedded with developers, not in a separate QA silo.
