Testing Lead for the Passenger app, with focus on microservices and backend reliability.

## Highlights

- **Consumer-Driven Contract Testing with Pact.io** — introduced and rolled out across services, replacing slow E2E coordination.
- **Mutation Testing with PITest** — introduced as a quality signal alongside line/branch coverage.
- **Business-flow checks through services** in Kotlin, JavaScript, and Python — replacing a slow E2E suite with targeted, fast checks at the right boundaries.
- **Workshops, training, and pairing sessions** to grow developer ownership of testing and quality — moving away from QA-as-handoff.
- **Service monitoring and deployment management.**
- **Code quality feedback via SonarQube** integrated into the dev loop.
- **Candidate interviews** — technical and agile mindset.
