Two layers can be rolled back independently:

1. SITE only (DNS stays at Cloudflare, point Pages records back at GitHub).
   Edit the A records for thatsabug.com in Cloudflare DNS to the four GitHub
   Pages IPs above, and the CNAME for www to joaogfarias.github.io. — that's
   all. No nameserver change needed. Fastest rollback.

2. FULL (back to GoDaddy DNS).
   At GoDaddy: Domains -> thatsabug.com -> Nameservers -> Change ->
   restore ns55.domaincontrol.com / ns56.domaincontrol.com. Re-enter all the
   records above in GoDaddy's DNS UI (the A records and CNAME for www are
   GoDaddy's defaults if no overrides exist; MX records may also be defaults
   for active GoDaddy email). Slower (24h propagation possible).

For most issues, option 1 is sufficient.
